# Charlie MJ Reader 1.1.6

- Preserved the restored 1.1.4.1 Medium/Freedium integration as the codebase.
- Removed the Saved Paragraphs item from the left sidebar while keeping paragraph capture and saved content available in See Saved.
- Moved See Saved between My Library and Bookmarks.
- Added responsive article cards and per-article saved-content categories in See Saved.
- Added Refresh & Fetch to article cards to update title, subtitle/description, thumbnail, author, publication date, canonical URL and readable article content from an already-open source tab.
- Added responsive grid treatment to Settings cards and controls.

Refresh & Fetch requires the source article tab to be open and accessible to the extension. Some websites restrict extraction or do not expose thumbnail/metadata.
