
## Read-aloud controls (manual Chrome verification)
- [ ] Open a saved article and select Read aloud.
- [ ] Confirm Play starts speech and the progress bar/word count advances.
- [ ] Confirm Pause stops speech without resetting progress.
- [ ] Confirm Play resumes from the paused word position.
- [ ] Confirm Stop cancels speech and resets progress to 0%.
- [ ] Reopen the article and confirm the saved progress is displayed.
- [ ] From the popup, select Listen and verify the popup media panel appears with Play/Pause/Stop.
